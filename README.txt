SANDEEP KUMAR — MODERN PERSONAL WEBSITE
========================================

Included:
- index.html (responsive one-page website)
- Dark charcoal + vivid orange visual theme
- Hyderabad exploration / photography section
- Movie articles and review section
- Video feature area
- About and contact sections
- Mobile navigation

PUBLISH FREE:
1. Extract this ZIP.
2. Open https://app.netlify.com/drop
3. Sign in / create a free account.
4. Drag the extracted folder (the folder containing index.html) onto Netlify Drop.
5. Netlify publishes it and gives you a public .netlify.app address.

CUSTOMIZE BEFORE SHARING:
- Search in index.html for hello@example.com and replace with your email.
- Replace the "YOUR PHOTO HERE" placeholder area with a photo you choose.
- Replace sample article copy and stock image URLs with your own photos and posts.
- Add your own Instagram and YouTube profile links.
- To add a YouTube video, you can later embed its iframe in the Videos section.

This is a static website; the starter can be hosted on Netlify's free plan.
A custom domain is optional and costs extra. External Google Fonts and Unsplash images require internet access.
